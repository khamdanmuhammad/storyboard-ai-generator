Quality & Anti-Artifact Guardrails Module 

REALISTIC CAMERA & PHYSICS MODULE:
Photorealistic commercial footage with physically plausible motion. Natural handheld micro-movement or stabilized gimbal movement. Realistic shutter motion blur. Natural autofocus behavior and subtle focus breathing. Realistic depth of field. Accurate perspective and object scale. Consistent shadows and reflections. Natural exposure and white balance. No rubbery skin, no waxy skin, no oversmoothed texture, no floating objects, no warped text, no changing product logo, no changing packaging.

Spesifikasi Sinematografi & Kamera

Detail setup kamera visual untuk menjamin tampilan konsisten dan realistis seperti iklan TV komersial.

Angle & Framing
  • Part 1: Eye-level 45° angle, medium shot transitioning to tight product reveal.
  • Part 2: Top-down macro & extreme close-up (ECU) focusing on hand interaction & texture.
  • Part 3: Medium chest-up shot of model with background bokeh, ending on stable hero product frame.
Lighting & Environment
  • Key Light: Soft warm window light at 45 degree angle (5600K balanced daylight).
  • Fill Light: Soft passive white bounce reflector to eliminate harsh shadows.
  • Color Grade: Natural commercial tones, rich contrast, true-to-life product color accuracy.
Camera Motion & Lens
  • Motion: Organic gimbal micro-panning, gentle push-in zoom (1.1x speed), continuous motion.
  • Lens: 35mm Prime f/1.8 for Part 1 & Part 3; 50mm Macro f/2.8 for Part 2 close-ups.
  • Shutter: 1/50s shutter speed at 24fps for natural motion blur.
Hand & Skin Realism
  • Hands: Natural Indonesian human skin texture, accurate finger articulation, physical contact with package.
  • Skin: Micro-pore details, natural sheen without waxy plastic AI artifacts.

Naskah Suara (Voiceover) & Subtitle Overlay

Intonasi percakapan natural Bahasa Indonesia dengan jeda singkat dan penekanan poin penting.
